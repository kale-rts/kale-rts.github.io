## Installation
You can subscribe to `Kales Multitasking Trainer` mod via:
1. [Online Mod Store](https://www.ageofempires.com/mods/details/550613/)
2. In-Game Mod Store

## Who is more likely to get something out of this?
1. You have completed the William Wallace campaign
2. You may have attempted/completed the Art of War challenges and/or some multiplayer games
3. You have enough experience with playing/watching the game to know you want to improve, but can't multitask well yet

## Description
In [the previous post]({ % post_url 2026-10-03-rts-fundamentals %}), we saw how a mental checklist helps us multitask. This is my attempt at defining one for AoE2 DE, with its practice tool.

### AoE2 Checklist
Here's the version we will be working on.
1. Check res bar
2. Check prod queue
3. Check minimap
4. Make decision

### Scenario Information

| Detail | Justification |
| --------------- | --------------- |
| 1v1 size map | Smallest map explorable in a reasonable time |
| Blank map | No map-specific context necessary |
| Random building/object spawns | Discourage mindless repetition |
| Automatic resource gathering | Force tight timings to avoid idle production |
| Villagers can't gather resources | No bypassing of tight timings |

### Win Condition
Explore all of the map. Do not fail any of the constraints of your difficulty level.

### Difficulty Information
Overcoming each level's constraint develops another vital skill for the player.

| Level | Constraint | Skill Developed |
| - | - | - |
| Easiest | Do not stop training villagers | Stronger economy |
| Standard | Do not stop moving your scout | Reading the game better |
| Moderate | Do not get housed | Stronger economy and military |
| Hard | Find all sheep around TC in time | Finding resources better |
| Hardest | Do not stop training militia | Simultaneous economy + army production | 
| Extreme | Set villager waypoints to specific resources | Avoiding idle/unbalanced economy |

### After Completion
You can now continue to improve your multitasking via:
1. **Endurance**: repeat the challenges in a bigger map to be able to multitask for longer
2. **Application**: flawless dark age via a build order (0s idle TC/eco, uptime hit, etc)

## Walkthrough

### General Considerations
1. Do not stop the mental checklist at all.
2. Keep your loop (specially execution) time windows as small as possible.
3. Do not skip difficulty levels.
4. Follow the mechanical optimization notes as specified.
5. Use [point and call](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointing_and_calling) to focus on each checklist item.

### Level 0: Easiest

#### Mechanical Considerations
* Encouraged
    * Train Villager hotkey (i.e. 'Q' with TC selected)
* Discouraged
    * Go To TC hotkey (hinders minimap awareness)
    * Control Groups (hinders minimap awareness)
    * Everything else
